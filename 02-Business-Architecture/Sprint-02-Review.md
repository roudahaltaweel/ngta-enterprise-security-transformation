# Sprint 2 Review

## Sprint Goal

The goal of Sprint 2 was to establish the Business Architecture of the National Government Technology Authority (NGTA). This sprint focused on understanding the organization's business operations before designing technical architecture and cybersecurity controls.

---

- Business Functions
- Business Services
- Business Stakeholders
- Critical Business Processes
- Critical Business Data
- User Groups

- Cybersecurity Planning
- GitHub Version Control

---

## Related Knowledge

This sprint applied concepts from:

- CompTIA Security+
- Enterprise Architecture
- Business Analysis
- Information Security Principles
- IT Security & Forensics

---

## Lessons Learned

Business architecture provides the foundation for enterprise cybersecurity. Before designing security controls, it is important to understand what the organization does, the services it provides, the people who use those services, and the critical information that must be protected.

---

## Challenges

The main challenge was maintaining consistency across multiple documents while ensuring that every business decision supported the organization's mission and future cybersecurity design.
