# Critical Business Processes
## Purpose

This document identifies the critical business processes of the National Government Technology Authority (NGTA). These processes are essential for delivering secure, reliable, and continuous technology services to UAE government entities.

Understanding these processes helps prioritize cybersecurity investments, business continuity planning, disaster recovery strategies, and operational resilience.

---

## Critical Business Processes

| Business Process | Description | Primary Department | Business Impact if Unavailable | Priority |
|------------------|-------------|--------------------|--------------------------------|----------|
| Government Service Delivery | Deliver secure technology services to government entities. | Information Technology | Government services become unavailable. | Critical |
| Cybersecurity Monitoring | Continuously monitor systems and respond to cyber threats. | Security Operations Center (SOC) | Increased risk of undetected cyberattacks. | Critical |
| Identity and Access Management | Authenticate users and manage access to systems and applications. | Cybersecurity | Users cannot securely access business systems. | Critical |
| Cloud Infrastructure Operations | Manage cloud platforms hosting government applications. | Cloud Services | Hosted applications become unavailable. | Critical |
| Network Operations | Maintain secure connectivity between offices, data centers, and cloud services. | Network Infrastructure | Communication between systems is interrupted. | Critical |
| Backup and Recovery | Perform regular backups and restore systems after failures or cyber incidents. | IT Operations | Permanent data loss and extended downtime. | Critical |
| Incident Response | Investigate and contain cybersecurity incidents. | Incident Response Team | Security incidents may spread and cause greater damage. | High |
| Governance, Risk and Compliance | Maintain security policies, risk management activities, and regulatory compliance. | GRC | Increased compliance and regulatory risk. | High |

---

## Business Need

NGTA relies on several critical business processes to deliver secure and reliable technology services to government entities. Identifying these processes helps the organization prioritize resources, improve operational resilience, and ensure that essential services remain available during disruptions.

---

## Security Benefit

Understanding critical business processes enables NGTA to identify high-value assets, prioritize cybersecurity controls, develop effective incident response procedures, and strengthen disaster recovery and business continuity planning.

---

## Assumptions

The following assumptions were made while identifying the critical business processes:

- Government technology services require high availability.
- Cybersecurity operations are performed continuously.
- Cloud platforms host critical government systems.
- Business continuity and disaster recovery are strategic priorities for NGTA.

---

## Summary

The critical business processes identified in this document represent the essential operational activities required to support NGTA's mission. Protecting these processes will guide future risk assessments, security architecture, business continuity planning, and enterprise resilience throughout the Enterprise Security Transformation Program.
