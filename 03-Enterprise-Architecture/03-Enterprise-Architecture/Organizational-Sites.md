# Organizational Sites

## Executive Summary

This document defines the physical operating locations of the National Government Technology Authority (NGTA). The selected model supports centralized governance, operational resilience, and secure service delivery while remaining appropriate for a medium-sized UAE federal government authority.

The organizational site strategy is based on international best practices, lessons learned from leading digital governments, and the UAE's digital transformation direction.

---

# Design Decision Summary

**Decision ID:** EA-001

**Topic:** Organizational Sites

### Problem

NGTA requires an organizational site model that supports secure government operations, business continuity, and efficient service delivery without unnecessary operational complexity.

### Options Considered

### Option 1 – Single Headquarters

**Advantages**
- Lower operating costs
- Simplified management
- Easier communication

**Disadvantages**
- Single point of failure
- Limited disaster recovery capability
- Reduced operational resilience

---

### Option 2 – Headquarters with Disaster Recovery Site (Selected)

**Advantages**
- Strong business continuity
- Improved disaster recovery
- Better operational resilience
- Centralized governance
- Cost-effective

**Disadvantages**
- Higher infrastructure cost than a single site
- Requires replication between locations

---

### Option 3 – Multiple Regional Offices

**Advantages**
- Regional presence
- Local operational support

**Disadvantages**
- Increased operational complexity
- Higher cost
- Additional cybersecurity management requirements
- Limited benefit for NGTA's mission

---

# Selected Solution

NGTA will operate from two primary locations:

- Headquarters – Abu Dhabi
- Disaster Recovery Site – Dubai

This model provides an appropriate balance between resilience, operational efficiency, security, and cost.

---

# Organizational Sites

## Headquarters – Abu Dhabi

The headquarters serves as the primary operational and strategic location for NGTA.

### Primary Functions

- Executive Leadership
- Enterprise Architecture
- Cybersecurity Governance (GRC)
- Security Operations Center (SOC)
- Network Operations Center (NOC)
- Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- Primary Data Center
- Core IT Operations
- Digital Forensics
- Incident Response Coordination

---

## Disaster Recovery Site – Dubai

The Disaster Recovery (DR) site provides operational continuity during major disruptions affecting the primary headquarters.

### Primary Functions

- Disaster Recovery Infrastructure
- Business Continuity Operations
- Backup Data Storage
- Secondary Security Operations Capability
- Critical Application Recovery
- Emergency Operations Workspace

---

# Business Justification

Abu Dhabi was selected as the headquarters because it is the UAE's federal government capital and the center for national strategic decision-making. This supports close alignment with federal initiatives and enables centralized governance.

Dubai was selected as the Disaster Recovery site because it provides geographic separation while maintaining excellent infrastructure, connectivity, and accessibility. The separation between the primary and recovery locations strengthens organizational resilience and supports business continuity planning.

---

# Security Justification

The selected site model improves NGTA's cybersecurity posture by:

- Reducing single points of failure
- Supporting disaster recovery and business continuity
- Enabling resilient government service delivery
- Improving incident response readiness
- Supporting secure replication of critical systems
- Strengthening operational resilience during cyber incidents

---

# Alignment with International Best Practices

The organizational site strategy is informed by:

- UAE federal digital government initiatives
- Singapore's GovTech operating model
- United States Zero Trust and resilience principles
- United Kingdom National Cyber Security Centre practices
- Estonia's digital government resilience model

Rather than maintaining numerous regional offices, these organizations emphasize centralized governance, resilient infrastructure, secure digital services, and effective continuity planning.

---

# Conclusion

NGTA adopts a two-site organizational model consisting of a headquarters in Abu Dhabi and a Disaster Recovery site in Dubai. This design provides strong governance, operational resilience, and cybersecurity while remaining scalable for future organizational growth.
